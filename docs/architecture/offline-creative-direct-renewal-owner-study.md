## Question

Is there one materially simpler, source-backed complete owner for the retained
PaulX-like neutral `Dream` target after diffusive, cyclic, renewal, and linked
STN closure?

## Evidence

Pinned PaulXStretch remains the only complete public path that directly owns
the preferred sound:

- one fractional source accumulator
- one long-window magnitude analysis
- input-phase removal
- fresh stochastic phase per output frame
- adjacent-frame raised-cosine combination with amplitude-modulation
  compensation
- separate channel engines under one shared source schedule

Its retained default does not require a transient detector, sinusoidal tracker,
material separator, iterative spectrogram inversion, or learned model.

Signal already reached the target sound with this family. Batch 31.25 passed
construction, structural, synthetic, and concealed mono admission; the operator
scored all `15` mono rows as ties against PaulXStretch. Later speaker review
called the stereo output solid and identified bounded, concrete differences:
channel balance, minor low-frequency noise, and opposite entry/tail energy
weighting. Those are product-relevant risks. They are not evidence that the
PaulX-like synthesis core missed its target.

There is no unused fifth family. Every source-backed alternative either owns a
different character or reintroduces more state than linked STN. Calling direct
magnitude renewal a new family would be dishonest. It is the same family that
already reached accepted long-form sound and then closed under mismatched or
incompletely retained objective authority.

## Authorized Product Reset

The operator authorized reopening. Batch 31.65 freezes one fresh
`DirectRenewalDream` brief. It is a clean-room product reset, not recovery of a
deleted candidate.

The renderer shape stays deliberately small:

1. One exact target lattice and monotonic fractional output-to-source map.
2. One fixed-support rolling source history and long-window magnitude view.
3. Deterministic frame-addressed stochastic phase renewal; no input-phase
   propagation, peak tracking, component separation, or transient state
   machine in neutral `Dream`.
4. One adjacent-frame combination and normalization law.
5. Native-channel magnitude analysis under shared scheduling, windowing,
   source position, and normalization. `space=0` uses one common excitation
   field; increasing `space` introduces deterministic channel decorrelation.
6. Zero exterior, explicit start/tail ownership, exact target crop, bounded
   duration-independent working state, and deterministic offline execution.

This shape follows the pinned source mechanism while keeping Signal-owned
mapping, stereo semantics, boundaries, state, and validation. No upstream
expression, constants, thresholds, tables, random generator, or control flow
transfers.

## Required Gate Change

Contract `085` Rule 11 prohibited another renewal candidate until an explicit
product-gate change addressed the prior failure classes. Batch 31.65 makes
that change and binds the complete renderer and evidence authority before any
candidate code exists.

Keep terminal:

- exact length, finiteness, deterministic identity, bounded working state, and
  zero exterior
- monotonic source progression and complete frame coverage
- no clip, discontinuity, click, output-support dropout, static freeze, or
  audible doubled attack/stutter
- complete whole-render and three-band channel-energy evidence against both
  source and PaulX; no unexplained channel-dominance reversal
- concealed long-form mono comparison at `4x`, `8x`, and `16x`
- comparator-relative stereo review, with an eligible independent listener
  required for promotion

Make diagnostic:

- cents error, crest growth, exact impulse-region count, and the `-30 dB`
  region boundary
- exact time-domain swap, polarity, and anti-phase algebra for decorrelated
  creative output
- mapped local-window source balance and dominance
- low-frequency noise, entry/tail energy weighting, width, correlation, and
  seed sensitivity

Diagnostics remain complete and may reject through listening. They do not veto
a candidate merely for differing from transparent reconstruction or exact
sample algebra that pinned PaulXStretch does not own. An audible replica,
stutter, image pull, or arbitrary level shift still rejects.

## Why This Is The Best Remaining Bet

- It targets the reference mechanism directly.
- Signal has already produced accepted mono and near-accepted stereo sound from
  the family.
- It removes the separator, tracker, event arena, residual model, and large
  evidence matrix that made linked STN hard to complete honestly.
- Its remaining risks match what the operator actually heard: low-end noise,
  stereo balance, and boundary-energy distribution.
- A failed fresh checkpoint would answer the product question cleanly. It
  would not launch another repair queue.

## Risks

- stochastic phase can still create seed-local crest or weak replicas
- native-channel renewal can move stereo correlation or local image
- long support can soften entry energy and over-weight the final crop
- magnitude-only synthesis can add low-frequency noise or reduce tonal focus
- one seed cannot prove the public `seed` range; multi-seed review remains
  post-promotion work
- stereo review remained a product decision; Batch 31.66 later closed it with
  an explicit checkpoint-scoped operator waiver rather than an independent
  listening claim
