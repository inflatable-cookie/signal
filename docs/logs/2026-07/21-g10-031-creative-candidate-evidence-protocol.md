freeze one clean acoustic checkpoint. Every later stage uses that exact tree
and is terminal on failure. Listening remains promotion authority.

One local-only `refs/signal-evidence/creative/<family>/<checkpoint>` ref keeps
exact source and test bodies available through required reassessment. Rejected
worktrees, branches, builds, and renders are deleted. The evidence ref is
deleted when reassessment closes and never enters `main` or push authority.

Conformance-only families may receive one explicit docs-only eligibility
decision under the new protocol. Synthetic or listening failures remain closed
unless new complete architecture or an evidence-backed product-gate change
addresses the failure. No family reopened in this batch.

No DSP, test, harness, API, route, cache, product, Loophole, or Chorus surface
changed.
