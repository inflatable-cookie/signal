# g10.031 Renewal Tonal-Coherence Closure

Date: 2026-07-20
Batch: 31.32
Status: complete; renewal family closed without promotion

## Decision

Close the Signal renewal family. Keep the PaulX-like `Dream` target and
comparator pack.

Batch 31.29 failed two `4x` tonal rows under seed `17`. Batch 31.31 failed the
`8x` chord under the audited admission seed. Different seeds, sources, and
ratios expose one mechanism class: magnitude-only stochastic frame renewal has
no persistent phase or oscillator state that can own finite-render tonal pitch.

A different seed, window, hop, transform, blend, threshold, or scalar does not
change that boundary. Adding phase recurrence, tracked peaks, oscillators,
material separation, or learned synthesis selects a different family.

## Source Check

- Signalsmith supplies coherent weighted phase prediction, but its public
  implementation describes best quality at modest ratios and randomizes
  observation distance above `2x` as an acknowledged fallback.
- Bungee and Rubber Band return to prohibited peak, multiresolution, or
  material-state work.
- pinned SBSMS already failed Signal's complete mono and linked-stereo
  feasibility controls.
- STN noise morphing and neural STN use separated output owners or trained
  state rather than one bounded first-party linked renderer.
- TSM-Net publishes pretrained inference, but not a released training path,
  usable repository licence, or intrinsic pitch and linked-stereo law.
- public TSM toolboxes expose the classical PV, WSOLA, and HPSS families already
  assessed.

No complete source-backed candidate remains ready. Reopening requires new
whole-system evidence for tonal coherence, musical diffusion at `4x`, `8x`,
and `16x`, linked channels, exact length, determinism, and bounded state, or an
explicit operator change to a governing product boundary.
