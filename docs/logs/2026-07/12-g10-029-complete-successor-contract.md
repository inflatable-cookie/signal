## Decision

Signal's next successor is one studied simultaneous multi-window system. It is
not another fixed-grid phase-vocoder variant, one-window selector, additive
component renderer, or independent branch hybrid.

The system owns:

1. linked continuous offline evidence
2. separate exact-point selection
3. one monotonic locally adaptive schedule with exact global closure
4. simultaneous short, medium, and long analysis layers
5. actual-hop phase transport
6. selected event-phase correction
7. cross-resolution vertical phase alignment
8. one output-side canonical-dual synthesis

## Why This Differs

The prior oracle selected one window at a time and prohibited event reset and
local timing. The new union keeps all resolutions concurrently. Study remains
observable when application is disabled. Timing, event phase, and vertical
phase are separately testable stages but are tuned only as a complete render.

## Tuning Boundary

The complete grid contains exactly `108` configurations. Hard numerical and
structural gates remove invalid renders. Objective quality fields form a Pareto
frontier but do not choose the winner. At most three candidates reach concealed
nine-row development listening. One frozen candidate may reach the six-row
holdout. Holdout never feeds tuning.

This replaces the earlier pattern of one untuned candidate followed by another
algorithm family. It also prevents open-ended parameter churn.
