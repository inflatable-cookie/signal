# Stretch Corpus v1

Status: active
Updated: 2026-07-07
Scope: `g10.021` first real stretch corpus manifest

## Purpose

This manifest names the material families required before Signal treats
OfflineHighQuality stretch evidence as more than a fast synthetic gate. It
defines the corpus shape and source policy only; it does not include source
audio.

## Source Policy

- Synthetic cases are generated by Signal and require no file I/O.
- Licensed listening material is operator-provided outside the repository.
- Do not commit licensed source audio.
- External benchmark renders are optional clean-room comparison outputs, never
  source material.
- Missing licensed listening material is reported as a gap and skipped by the
  future real-corpus runner.

## Corpus Defaults

- manifest id: `stretch-corpus-v1`
- schema version: `1`
- sample rate: `48000`
- channels: `2`
